## Visual direction

- Keep the existing personal portrait across all three platforms.
- Use one restrained banner system rather than three unrelated graphics.
- Preserve the established technical visual language: dark navy field, white
  typography, blue tensor structures, and limited orange accents.
- Banner message: `Deep Learning, All the Way Down`
- Supporting line: `From first principles in modern C++`
- Avoid episode numbers, upload schedules, platform logos, excessive code, and
  generic AI imagery.
- Generate platform-specific crops only after capturing each platform's current
  safe areas and mobile behavior.
