ReLU has a corner at zero, so its derivative isn't defined there. Our backward rule uses zero at that point, which is a convention rather than a derivative we can calculate.

It also means an input at or below zero receives no gradient through this ReLU operation. Tanh and sigmoid have different slopes, so swapping the forward function also changes how the model learns.
