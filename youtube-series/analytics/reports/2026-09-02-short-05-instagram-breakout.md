## What the result supports

Instagram supplied a much larger cold-distribution test than any prior Reel on
the account. The audience also consumed and acted on the content after seeing
it. The 17.57-second average watch time on a 19.77-second video, 275 saves, 134
shares, and 85 comments show that the result is not explained by reach alone.

The Short has a simple first-second promise, visible code, progressive reveal,
a quantified payoff, and a final technical question. This combination is the
strongest current creative template for another controlled test.

## What the result does not prove

- It does not isolate publication time. Short 5 was published at 8:26 AM ET,
  but topic, hook, runtime, visual treatment, payoff, and ending also changed.
- It does not prove that a specific hashtag caused distribution.
- It does not prove that the final question alone caused the comment count.
- It does not show that every new Instagram follower came from this Reel.
- It does not explain YouTube's limited distribution of the same Short.
