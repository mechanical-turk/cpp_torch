## Interpretation

1. Episode 1 converts a small qualified audience unusually well into likes and
   subscribers. The concept and series promise appear to resonate.
2. Most early reach is external, direct, or unclassified. YouTube-native
   discovery is not established yet.
3. The processed retention average is weak for a 37-minute lesson. The exact
   cause cannot be diagnosed until the audience-retention curve arrives, but
   the opening, expectation-setting, and time-to-first-payoff deserve scrutiny.
4. Search volume is tiny but its watch time per view is promising. Titles and
   descriptions should continue using precise problem-oriented language.
5. Episode 2 has a weaker early view trajectory than Episode 1. Its earlier
   22-impression, 0% CTR snapshot is too small for a packaging verdict, but it
   warrants monitoring rather than a thumbnail change today.
6. Short 1 is too new to judge. Its Shorts-specific retention and viewed-versus-
   swiped-away metrics should be reviewed after at least 24 hours.
