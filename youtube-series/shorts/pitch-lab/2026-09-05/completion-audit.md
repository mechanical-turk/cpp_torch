## Editorial audit and limits

The rounds contain different experiments, not repeated hooks for one topic.
Some adjacent ideas share a broader theme, such as ownership or compiler work;
their critiques explicitly account for that repetition risk.

The observed Instagram opening loss and comment threads inform review criteria.
They do not establish the demand, retention, reach or follower conversion of
these unposted concepts. No new analytics was pulled and no causal audience
finding was manufactured from the exercise.

Technical references support the underlying mechanisms. Measurement-dependent
claims remain conditional on the specific benchmark and code checks in each
file. This task did not build or benchmark twenty demos, render twenty openings,
record narration, research platform hashtag demand, or approve any upload.
Those are production gates, not missing steps in the requested pitch-review loop.

The shortlist is an editorial next-action recommendation. It does not certify
any candidate as perfect or guarantee a performance result.
