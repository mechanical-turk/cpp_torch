Episode announcements are only one part of the account. On most active days,
publish one or two native personal or process observations; publish more only
when there is something genuine to say. These posts should usually contain no
external link or promotional call to action.

Rotate among:

- the emotional reality of recording and editing alone;

- concise technical opinions or questions;
- mistakes, corrections, and moments when an abstraction finally becomes
  intuitive;
- meaningful production milestones and occasional understated humor.
