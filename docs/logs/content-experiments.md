## 2026-09-04: Credibility constraint on deliberate disagreement

- Learner clarification: the approach must be careful and calculated. It must
  preserve technical credibility and avoid appearing manipulative,
  inexperienced, or amateur.
- Add a required credibility check: understand the omitted nuance, preserve the
  meaning of the claim in its visible context, show competent experiments, and
  prepare an informed response to the expected objection.
- Evaluate discussion quality alongside engagement. Repeated confusion about
  basic correctness or undisclosed decisive test conditions is a reason to
  revise the presentation, even when the comment count rises.
