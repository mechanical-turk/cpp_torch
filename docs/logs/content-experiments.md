  preserve technical credibility and avoid appearing manipulative,
  inexperienced, or amateur.

  prepare an informed response to the expected objection.

  basic correctness or undisclosed decisive test conditions is a reason to
  revise the presentation, even when the comment count rises.

### 2026-10-26: Chapter 15 phase 1c plan: does momentum's advantage grow with batch size? (plan; written before results)

- Why: literature (Kidambi et al. 2018; Dang et al. 2024; Shallue et al. 2018 and Zhang
  et al. 2019 on critical batch size; classical heavy-ball analysis for full-batch
  quadratics) says momentum's step advantage over SGD is limited by gradient noise at small
  batches and should appear at larger ones. Every run so far used batch 64. This tests that
  prediction on our network; it does not assume it holds.
- Network: the Chapter 14 network (tanh, residual, 16 hidden layers, width 32, series
  initialization). Same data (first 10,000 training images in file order, wrapped around when a
  run needs more than one pass), same validation images (10,000-14,999).
- Protocol change: a fixed budget of 3,000 optimizer steps for every batch size, constant
  learning rate (no decay, so batch sizes are comparable), evaluation every 20 steps on
  validation, the first 2,000 test images, and the full 10,000 training images (loss and accuracy).
- Batch sizes: 16, 64, 256, 1,024 and 10,000 (full batch).
- Arms: plain SGD, learning rates 0.003, 0.005, 0.007, 0.01, 0.014, 0.02, 0.03, 0.04, 0.06,
  0.08, 0.12, 0.16, 0.24; SGD with momentum 0.9, learning rates 0.0003, 0.0005, 0.0007, 0.001,
  0.0014, 0.002, 0.003, 0.004, 0.006, 0.008, 0.012, 0.016, 0.024. Spacing about 1.4x, chosen
  because coarser grids misled phases 1 and 1b. If an arm's best rate is the largest or
  smallest in its grid, that arm is extended and rerun before any comparison is read.
- Seeds 42 and 1 (initialization).
- Targets: primary, validation accuracy 92% (same as phase 1); secondary, validation accuracy
  90% and 93%, and training loss 0.1 on the full 10,000 training images. Steps to target as
  before (three consecutive evaluations). A target a run never reaches is "not reached".
- Selection: per batch size and arm, the learning rate with the lowest mean steps to the
  primary target over the two seeds.
- Hypothesis and rule, fixed now: the literature-based hypothesis is that momentum's saving in
  steps grows with batch size. It counts as supported if, at batch 10,000, momentum needs at
  least 33% fewer steps than plain SGD to reach 92% on both seeds, and the saving at 1,024 and
  at 10,000 each exceeds the saving at batch 64 by at least 15 percentage points. Otherwise
  the result is "not supported on this network". If it looks supported, confirm on fresh seeds
  3-7 with the best learning rate of each arm plus its two neighbours before using it.
