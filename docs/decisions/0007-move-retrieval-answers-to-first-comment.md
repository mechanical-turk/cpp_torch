# Decision 0007 - Move Retrieval Answers to the First Comment

## Status

Accepted.

## Context

Every episode ends with a small set of retrieval questions. The original format
answered those questions near the beginning of the next episode. Initial
retention evidence shows that the opening minute is the most fragile part of the
lesson, and spoken retrieval delays the new problem for returning viewers while
adding prerequisite material before cold viewers understand the payoff.

## Decision

Beginning with Episode 3's published package:

- each episode ends with approximately three retrieval questions;
- the exact answers to those questions are published in that same episode's
  first pinned comment;
- the next episode begins with the required series opening, one economical
  recap, and its new problem and payoff; and
- the video opening does not contain a previous-question answer segment.

## Consequences

- New substance can begin earlier without abandoning retrieval practice.
- Viewers who attempted the questions retain an accessible answer key.
- Pinned comments become part of the educational artifact, not only navigation.
- Episode 3's pinned comment supplies its answer key, so Episode 4 can apply the
  new opening structure immediately.
