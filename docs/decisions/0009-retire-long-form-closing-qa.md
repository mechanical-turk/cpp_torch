# Decision 0009 - Retire Long-Form Closing Q&A

## Status

Accepted September 7, 2026. Applies from Episode 8 onward.
Supersedes the closing-question and answer-comment requirements in
[Decision 0007](0007-move-retrieval-answers-to-first-comment.md) and that portion
of [Decision 0006](0006-record-shorts-separately.md).

## Context and decision

The creator chose to stop the recurring end-of-episode Q&A. New long-form
episodes end with an itemized recap, a concise follow-up, the standard sign-off
and a clean end-screen tail. Do not prepare closing questions, answer keys or
automatic answer comments, and do not remind the creator to pin such a comment.

Keeping questions but moving their answers elsewhere was the previous approach;
this decision removes that recurring segment entirely. It is a creator
direction, not a measured claim that removing Q&A improves retention.

## Consequences and limits

- Episode 8 ends after the four arithmetic backward rules. Episode 9 covers
  sum and matmul backward, gradient tests and a linear regression training loop.
- Preserve historical scripts, media and published comments.
- Questions used to teach during the lesson remain allowed.
- Shorts discussion prompts and next-experiment endings are unchanged.
- Explicitly planned useful comments remain allowed; no comment is the default
  when the long-form package has none planned.
- Publishing and engine checks must accept a package without an answer comment.

The authoritative current ending is in the
[long-form playbook](../../youtube-series/production/long-form-playbook.md).
Revisit this decision only if the creator requests a new format or subsequent
long-form evidence supports testing a particular closing activity.
