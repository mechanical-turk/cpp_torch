# Decision 0008 - Make Episode Openings Standalone

## Status

Accepted.

## Context

Opening an episode by discussing "last episode" frames the lesson as dependent
on prior viewing. That can create unnecessary friction for a new viewer before
the current problem has earned their attention.

## Decision

- Episode openings explain the current problem directly and stand on their own.
- They do not say "last episode," "last time," or lead with a recap by default.
- If a prior concept is required, the script restates that concept economically
  without describing where or when it was previously taught.
- Explicit continuity is retained only when omitting it would make the current
  explanation materially confusing.

## Consequences

- A viewer can enter the series through any episode without being told they are
  behind.
- The fragile opening seconds are spent on the present idea and payoff.
- Series continuity remains available through titles, playlists, descriptions,
  pinned comments, and occasional necessary references later in the lesson.
