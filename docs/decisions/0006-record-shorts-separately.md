# Decision 0006 - Record Shorts Separately from Long-Form Episodes

## Status

Accepted.

## Context

Designing portions of a long-form 16:9 lesson for later vertical extraction
adds production constraints to the primary teaching medium. Code, diagrams,
narration, and transitions that serve a complete chapter do not necessarily
produce the strongest native short-form lesson.

## Decision

Long-form episode scripts will optimize only for coherent, rigorous 16:9
teaching. They will not contain clip boundaries, candidate Short titles,
vertical-safe composition notes, or intentionally self-contained extraction
modules.

Shorts will be selected, scripted, and recorded separately in native vertical
format. They may revisit an idea from a long-form episode, but each Short will
be designed as its own complete lesson rather than cut from the main recording.

The long-form learning loop is: concise cold open, brief previous-episode recap,
explicit current-episode roadmap, brisk answers to the previous questions,
concept-to-code-to-verification, review, concise next setup, and retrieval
questions as the final spoken content.

## Consequences

- Long-form explanations can use the full screen and natural conversational
  context without compromising for vertical reframing.
- Shorts require a separate scripting and recording pass.
- Repeated subject matter is acceptable when it is newly structured for the
  short-form medium.
- Short-form production remains part of the distribution strategy, but no
  longer shapes the long-form chapter script.
