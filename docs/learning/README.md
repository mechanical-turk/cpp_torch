# Learning Artifacts

These are distilled teaching records maintained continuously by Codex. They are
organized by phase, milestone, or detour and may contain explanations,
misconceptions, quiz prompts, experiments, and revision topics.

They distinguish:

- **Presented:** material explained during teaching;
- **Demonstrated:** understanding shown independently by the learner; and
- **Review:** material that should be retrieved or tested again.

These artifacts preserve teaching continuity but do not replace learner-authored
work required by a milestone mastery gate.
